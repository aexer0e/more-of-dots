More of Dots

A free app for War of Dots on Windows, macOS and Linux. Watch and export replays, follow the leaderboards, and build custom maps.

Download for Windows Watch replays in your browser

Also for Windows macOS Linux Source on GitHub

Free · Open source · v2.1.0

Features

  • Replay player

    Open any replay in its own window. Seek, change speed and toggle layers, with the game's own music and sounds.

  • Video export

    Turn replays into MP4 videos with sound, up to 1080p and much faster than real time.

  • Every map, every mode

    Replays open on all 80 official maps in 1v1, 2v2, free-for-all and experiment mode.

  • Replay browser

    Search by player, filter by mode and map, and keep a backup of every replay.

  • Leaderboards

    Track your rank and compare your progress with up to 10 other players.

  • Map editor

    Paint terrain, place units, and save custom maps to the game.
