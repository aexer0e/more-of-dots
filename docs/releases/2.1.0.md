# More of Dots 2.1.0

- More of Dots now runs on macOS with Apple silicon, and a Linux AppImage is available for playing War of Dots through Steam's Proton. The macOS app is not notarized: the first time you open it, confirm it under System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
- Every official map plays in every mode. Replays that record only a map number now open on all 80 maps the game ships, including the free-for-all maps and Zolamare 38 and 51 to 56, in 1v1, 2v2, free-for-all and experiment mode. A map added by a later game update takes its starting layout from any of your replays that records it.
- Video export draws and encodes the same way on every platform. Exports are as fast as before on Windows, and the bitrate is now an upper limit, so simple scenes make smaller files.
- Region selection stays Windows-only; on other platforms its tab is shown but cannot be opened.
- Save dialogs and Show in folder use the system's own dialogs on every platform.
- The hosted replay player at aexer0e.dev keeps the replays you open, shows tournament brackets with their replays, makes share links, and opens replays from links such as Discord attachments.

The starting layouts were read from the game's own map table, and every layout More of Dots already had matched it exactly. All number-only replays on the test machine opened, and those that previously had no layout played to their recorded ends with every order applied. On Windows, a 1080p export ran at 115 frames per second, against 114 for 2.0.2.
