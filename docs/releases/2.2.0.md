# More of Dots 2.2.0

- Share a replay from its card. The link button beside Play uploads it and copies a link that plays it at aexer0e.dev. A replay already shared, from the app or the site, gets the same link without another upload. Shared replays keep a green button, and Open in browser plays the link right away.
- Upload opens a dialog for dropping replays, choosing files or pasting a link, including Discord attachments and replay share links. Dropping .rep files anywhere on the Replays page also adds them.
- Number-only replays of maps 33, 37, 38 and 39 are now named and pictured as the Zolamare maps they are. Both official sets use these numbers, and these replays were shown as Fahero maps, with the Fahero picture and in the Fahero map filter. Playback was not affected.
