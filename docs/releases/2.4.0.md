# More of Dots 2.4.0

- The hosted replay player can continue any replay as a live match. Pause where the match should pick up, choose Play from here and send the room link to another player. Each side plays from that moment with fog of war and the game's controls, and a finished match can be downloaded as a replay.
- A new Nations tab shows the War of Dots Nations campaign: the current map, land and city control, each nation's players and economy, the top players on each side, and how the campaign has moved over the last day, week, month or all of it.
- Replays shared before a change to the card design get the new picture in their link previews.
