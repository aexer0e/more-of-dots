# More of Dots 2.4.1

- Front lines now run off the edge of the map, as they do in the game, so it is clear when an army is encircled. Before, two lines that met the same edge could be joined along it.
- Replays in Your replays have a download button next to the link button.
- Matches played with Play from here are added to Your replays when they end, and the victory and defeat screens can copy a link to the match or download it.
- The play page loads the current engine after a site update.
